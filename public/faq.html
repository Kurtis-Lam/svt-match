Getting Started

SVT MATCH is currently available on Android. Download the APK file from the link on our home page, then install it on your Android device.

You may need to enable "Install unknown apps" in your Android settings before installing.

An iOS version is currently in development. Stay tuned to the Updates page for announcements!

Gameplay
  • Daily Freebie and Boxes!
  • Leveling up in Minigames!
  • Completing Quests!
  • Marrying Idols to get Offline Rewards!

Idols come in different rarities, with the price of owning higher for higher rarity idols.

  • Rare – Total 5 Idols
  • Super Rare – Total 3 Idols
  • Epic – Total 2 Idols
  • Mythic – Total 2 Idols
  • Legendary – The one and only S.COUPS

You can challenge any of your friends to a minigame battle. Select a minigame, choose a friend from your friends list, and compete to show your strength!

Account & Technical

Please contact us via the Support page with your SVT username and email address. We'll help you regain access to your account as soon as possible.

Your game data is saved to the server, so it should be safe. If your progress seems missing, try logging out and back in. If the issue persists, contact us through the Support page and we'll investigate.

  • Make sure you have the latest version of the APK installed
  • Restart your device and try again
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • If the issue continues, submit a bug report via our Support page
